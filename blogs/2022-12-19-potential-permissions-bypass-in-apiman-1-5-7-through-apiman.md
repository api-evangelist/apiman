A vulnerability in Apiman has been disclosed that you need to be aware of and respond to. It has CVE ID CVE-2022-47551 . Details Incorrect default permissions for certain read-only resources in the Apiman 1.5.7.Final through 2.2.3.Final in the Apiman Manager REST API allows a remote authenticated attacker to access information and resources in an Apiman Organizations they are not a member of and/or do not have permissions for.
